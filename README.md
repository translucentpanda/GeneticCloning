# Genetic Cloning

Have a dino with perfect Gene Traits and find yourself dreading the thought of having to catch more of the same in order to get more copies? Even worse, is it a low spawn-rate dino like a Giga or Carchar?

Worry no more!

This bad luck protection mod adds a variation of the Tek Cloning Chamber that also copies Gene Traits over to the clone. There are no separate engrams or resource / fuel requirements; simply pick up your existing Cloning Chamber (or craft the original) and place it down as a Tek Genetic Cloning Chamber. Thanks to space magic, the Tek Genetic Cloning Chamber also clones dinos ~5 times faster than the original, right out of the box.

Cloned traits work with the Gene Scanner and Gene Storage from Bob's Tall Tales.

Everything you know and love about the existing Cloning Chamber, but now with Gene Traits and modifiable speed!

##### GameUserSettings.ini
- CloningSpeedMultiplier=0.2
